# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
-laundry shop owner who want to keep in track their order.
-customer who want to track their order status.

## What exactly is going wrong?
Traditional laundry businesses use paper tags and unstructured messaging apps. Traditional laundry businesses lose claim tickets and misplace garments. Traditional laundry businesses also suffer from communication errors regarding fabric care. Cannot track order queues. Customers do not have real‑time visibility into when their clothes will be ready, for pickup or delivery. Customers constantly inquire about status updates.

## Why does it matter?
Manual paper ticketing and fragmented communication cause up to 20% of errors. These errors lead to damaged clothes, late deliveries and money loss. Than 65% of customer support questions are about order status or care. These repeated questions create problems for support teams. They cause customers to leave when human help is not available, after regular business hours.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
Traditional laundry shops usually rely on paper tags and scattered chat apps, which causes lost claim tickets, misplaced clothing, and frequent mistakes. Customers get frustrated because they cannot easily check when their laundry will be ready. On top of that, shop staff spend most of their day answering the same simple questions about order updates and garment care. The aLaundry website solves these issues by putting everything into one digital system that automatically tracks orders, sends instant status updates, and provides a 24/7 AI chatbot to answer customer questions immediately.

# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
-Laundry shop owners who struggle to keep tracks their order manually.
-Customer who want to know about their laundry status.

## What exactly is going wrong?
Traditional laundry businesses rely on manual paper track and messaging apps will lead to mess where the order will not keep in track.This will make miscommunication as customer cannot know their laundry's status.

## Why does it matter?
Manual paper ticketing and fragmented communication can lead to operational error rates as high as 20%, causing damaged clothes, delayed deliveries and financial losses. Over 65% of customer support requests are repetitive order status or care questions, causing severe support bottlenecks and driving customer churn during non-business hours when human staff is unavailable.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
Traditional laundry shops usually rely on paper tags and scattered chat apps, which causes lost claim tickets, misplaced clothing, and frequent mistakes. Customers get frustrated because they cannot easily check when their laundry will be ready. On top of that, shop staff spend most of their day answering the same simple questions about order updates and garment care. The aLaundry website solves these issues by putting everything into one digital system that automatically tracks orders, sends instant status updates, and provides a 24/7 AI chatbot to answer customer questions immediately.
